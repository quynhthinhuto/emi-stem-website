  1. 1Phenomenon and concept

  2. 2Learning objectives

    Write objectives for conceptual understanding, measurement, calculation, and English explanation.

  3. 3Key language

    Prepare terms for variables, units, cause and effect, comparison, prediction, and evidence.

  4. 4Activity sequence

    Guide students through prediction, observation, data collection, calculation, discussion, and conclusion.

  5. 5Assessment

    Check concept accuracy, use of units, interpretation of evidence, and oral or written explanation in English.
